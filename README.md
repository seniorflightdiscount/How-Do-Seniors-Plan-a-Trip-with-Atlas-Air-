<h2>Understand How Atlas Air Operates</h2>
Before any planning is made as far as traveling is concerned, it is important that the senior citizens realize that Atlas Air does not provide any commercial flights on a scheduled basis. If eligible for travel with Atlas Air through charter or tours, ensure that all of your travel needs are taken care of ahead of time.

<h2>Confirm Your Travel Eligibility</h2>
The first thing to do  is to determine whether you have eligibility to travel through the particular Atlas Air flight. The type of the flight operation will dictate which documentation that passengers require from the charterer, employer, government organization, or travel firm.

<h2>Booking through the Proper Organization</h2>
As there is not a passenger reservation system for Atlas Air, it will normally be made by the organizing party. Seniors need to:
• Verify the flight dates and timings.
• Check their baggage allowance.
• Ask for seat details.
• Ask about special arrangements if any.

<h2>Ask for Mobility Assistance Promptly</h2>
If you need any more help at the airport, please inform your travel coordinator immediately. Mobility services that can assist elderly travelers who have difficulty moving around include:
• Assistance for using a wheelchair
• Assistance during boarding and disembarking the plane
• Priority assistance at the airport
• Extra time for boarding

<h2>Important Travel Documents Preparation</h2>
It is important that you have all your documents with you prior to travelling to the airport and these include:
• Identity card/Passport
• Ticket confirmation
• Medical documents (if any)
• Medicines prescribed by a doctor
• Emergency contacts
In case you have to travel to a foreign land, make sure that your passport and visa are valid.

<h2>Pack Smart</h2>
Make sure to put all your necessities in the hand luggage, such as:
• Medicine
• Eyeglasses/earphones
• Power Bank for your cell phone
• Food
• Re-Usable Water Bottle (it can be refilled post-security check)
• Sweatshirt or travel blanket
• Documents/paperwork
It is very helpful to have everything handy.

<h2>Arrive at the Airport Early</h2>
It is important for travelers from the older generation to report in advance of the minimum required check-in time since it allows enough time for:
• Security clearance
• Check-in
• Assistance in moving around
• Boarding gate identification
• Any unforeseen problems

<h2>Make Yourself Comfortable During Your Flight</h2>
Older travelers can find long flights to be draining. In order to make yourself comfortable during your flight:
• Drink plenty of fluids.
• If you can, stretch your legs at intervals.
• Make sure that you are wearing comfortable clothes.
• Bring neck pillows and lightweight blankets.
Simple preparations can make long journeys much more enjoyable.

<h2>Review Baggage Rules</h2>

• Allowances for carry-on luggage
• Allowed amount of checked luggage
• Charges for oversized luggage
• Guidelines for medical equipment
This will prevent any surprises when boarding your flight.
